Run the local Minikube deploy script from PowerShell:

```powershell
.\apply-deploy.ps1
.\apply-deploy.ps1 -DeployTestBackend
.\apply-deploy.ps1 -RunTestLoadTests
.\apply-deploy.ps1 -DeployProductionBackend
.\apply-deploy.ps1 -RunLoadTests
.\apply-deploy.ps1 -PortForward
.\apply-deploy.ps1 -RecreateBackend
```
